Service Coverage and Time Slots

Blue Stream Group delivers orders within active service zones based on checkout address validation and available operational capacity.

Orders are confirmed after successful placement and payment status verification. The delivery team receives assignment and routing details from the operations system.

Failed Delivery and Reattempts

If a recipient is unavailable at delivery time, our team may attempt contact using the provided phone number. Reattempt and rescheduling options depend on route capacity and product type.
