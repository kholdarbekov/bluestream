Pages label the value and never work the wait out. - order_status_label(code): the words. The wait keeps its own sentence; every other status is `web.cabinet.status.` (scripts/seed_web_ui_cabinet_translations.py). Before that seed has run (a deploy window) a status reads as its own name, "Out for delivery", never as a raw key. - ORDER_STATUS_TONES: the colour (.ae-status tone): placed and being prepared are blue, on the way is navy, delivered is green, waiting for a new date is amber, cancelled and returned are grey (the subscription page's chips use the same colours for the same outcomes). - order_status_pill(code): label + tone as a pill.
